1. Why MHA wins on PPL (292.21 vs 293.62)

    6 independent heads → each head learns different attention patterns
    More degrees of freedom to distribute knowledge
    MQA's single KV head bottlenecks information sharing across query heads

2. Why MQA wins on speed (-27% vs MHA)
    1 KV head instead of 6 → 6x fewer KV computations
    Formula: wei @ v done once, broadcast 6 times (vs 6 independent)
    Memory bandwidth: critical bottleneck in attention, not FLOP count

3. Why GQA has best generalization (gap=0.6308)
    Grouped heads (3 groups of 2) = "sweet spot"
    Enough independence (not too sparse like MQA)
    Enough sharing (not too independent like MHA)
    Prevents overfitting better than both extremes

4. Why MLHA is slowest (~10.9 min)
    Latent mixing layer adds extra linear transformations
    PPL worse (297.16) = overhead not justified by this dataset size

5. Why training time doesn't perfectly correlate with parameters
    MHA: 6.33M params, 13.7 min
    GQA: 6.03M params, 10.3 min
    Difference = attention compute, not embedding parameters
    GQA's grouped attention uses less memory bandwidth despite similar param count
